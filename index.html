WHY THIS CALL
    THE PROBLEM

    SAIL books ocean freight with limited visibility into what's actually driving the price.

    01

    Unpredictable cost

    Spot prices, bunker fuel, and port congestion move independently — and often against each other.

    02

    Poor vessel choice

    Availability, draft restrictions, and berth compatibility rule out ships after time is already spent evaluating them.

    03

    Rush booking

    Late decisions default to premium pricing, demurrage exposure, and delivery slippage.

    04

    Uncertain calls

    Too many variables move at once for a booking manager to weigh by hand, every time, at scale.

    HOW IT WORKS

    From a shipment request to an approved booking, in four steps.

    INPUT

    Read the market

    Demand, vessel availability, freight & fuel indices, port and geopolitical conditions.

    ANALYZE

    Score the options

    Price trend direction, vessel fit, port constraints, seasonal risk — computed together, not in isolation.

    DECIDE

    Recommend a position

    Lock now, wait, or partial-lock — with the timing that minimizes downside.

    EXPLAIN

    Show the reasoning

    Expected cost, downside risk, confidence, and the specific signals behind the call.
